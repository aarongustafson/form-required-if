form-required-if via unpkg

This page loads the guarded definition helper from unpkg and registers the element when supported. See the README.

Select Email, then provide an email address. If JavaScript is unavailable or the module fails to load, the fields still work: follow that instruction yourself. Production forms must also validate on the server. This demo does not send your details.

Guarded automatic registration

<label for="contact-method">Preferred contact method</label>
<select id="contact-method" name="contact-method">
  <option value="">Select one</option>
  <option value="email">Email</option>
  <option value="phone">Phone</option>
</select>
<form-required-if conditions="contact-method=email" indicator=" (required)">
  <label for="email">Email (required when Email is selected)</label>
  <input type="email" id="email" name="email" aria-describedby="email-required" />
  <p class="required-message" id="email-required">Required when Email is selected: provide your email address.</p>
</form-required-if>
